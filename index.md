I am an Associate Professor at the [Department of Mathematics and Computer Science](https://www.sdu.dk/en/om-sdu/institutter-centre/imada_matematik_og_datalogi) at the [University of Southern Denmark (SDU)](https://www.sdu.dk/en), where I lead the [AI Safety & Interpretability Lab](https://aisilab.github.io) and co-lead [OdenseNLP](https://odensenlp.github.io). I am also an affiliated member of the [Pioneer Centre for Artificial Intelligence (P1)](https://www.aicentre.dk), a member of the [Digital Democracy Centre](https://www.sdu.dk/en/forskning/forskningsenheder/samf/digitaldemocracycentre), and a member of the [International Association for Safe and Ethical AI](https://www.iaseai.org).

My research advances AI safety by understanding how and why AI models behave the way they do -- and by developing methods to keep that behavior legible and controllable. Specifically, my research areas comprise **interpretability**, **emergent communication**, and **multi-agent LLM systems**. One line of my work studies model internals to identify the functional roots of model behavior, using mechanistic interpretability and experimental paradigms from psycholinguistics ([calibration of activation oracles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26045), [culture neurons](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-ijcnlp.45/)). In another line of work, I study the interaction *between* models -- how communication emerges in populations of agents, and why some communication protocols stay readable while others drift away from human language ([compositionality in language learning](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-55158-1), [emergent languages in LLM agent populations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.31170)). These strands converge on the key question of how to retain legibility in machine reasoning and communication, and at what cost ([auditability vs. accuracy](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1644/)).

I am the principal investigator of the [MIST project](https://aisilab.github.io/mist/) on scalable mechanistic interpretability for safe and trustworthy LLM agents, funded by the Novo Nordisk Foundation, and I lead the alignment workstream in the [Danish Foundation Models project](https://www.foundationmodels.dk/), where we train open multilingual models that excel at Danish using only permissible data ([DFM Mimir v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13517)) -- contributing to European AI sovereignty.
